Privacy

A plain-language note on what this site collects and why.

What's collected

This site uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use it - which pages people read, how far they scroll, and where they click. Clarity records anonymized session replays and heatmaps, and sets cookies to recognize repeat visits.

Clarity doesn't collect your name or contact details, and text on the page is masked in recordings. I don't sell or share this data, and I don't use it for advertising.

Why

I work in search, and this site is partly a lab. Seeing how real people use it helps me make it better and test ideas.

Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker, and the site will still work normally. For more detail, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
