Privacy

A plain-language note on what this site collects and why.

What's collected

This site uses two analytics tools to understand how visitors use it:

  • Google Analytics measures visits - which pages people read, how they found the site, and general details like device type and approximate location.
  • Microsoft Clarity shows how people use each page - how far they scroll and where they click - through anonymized session replays and heatmaps.

Both set cookies to recognize repeat visits.

Neither tool collects your name or contact details, and text on the page is masked in Clarity recordings. I don't sell or share this data, and I don't use it for advertising.

Why

I work in search, and this site is partly a lab. Seeing how real people use it helps me make it better and test ideas.

Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker, and the site will still work normally. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. For more detail, see how Google uses data from sites that use its services and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
