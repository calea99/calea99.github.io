SEO / GEO / AEO

Curious by nature. Dedicated by habit.

I'm Carolyn Wilborn. I lead SEO and GEO strategy for ecommerce and B2B brands, making sure people and AI systems can find, read, and trust their content. I bring the same curiosity to every problem - whether it's a stubborn technical issue or a page that should be ranking and isn't.

One example

The problem
A business wasn't showing up in AI answers, but when the owner asked a chatbot, it insisted that the site was accessible.
What I did
I ran their pages through a side-by-side tool I built, which shows a page the way a person sees it next to the way a non-rendering AI crawler sees it. We then screenshared and asked the chatbot a more specific question: can it see the products listed on the page? The real answer: no, because the LLM couldn't render JavaScript.
The result
They moved from client-side to server-side rendering, and became an SEO client.
